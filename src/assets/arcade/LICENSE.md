# Artwork provenance

The player and ghosts are high-resolution remasters of the original project
sprites, created with the built-in imagegen tool. They preserve the classic
silhouettes, expressions and colors. Transparent cells were extracted into
314×314 PNGs; the other player directions are rotations of the right-facing one.

The original source sprites remain in the repository history under
`src/game/assets/pacman/` and `src/game/assets/ghosts/`.
Generation prompts and integration notes are in `project-management/ARTWORK.md`.
The UI background and maze walls are rendered from code at native resolution.
No downloaded third-party asset pack is used by the current game.
